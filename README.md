## Getting Started

### **Step 1: Register Your Hotel**
1. Visit [Jhattse Business](https://business.jhattse.com)
2. Click "Get Started" or "Free Trial"
3. Enter your hotel details
4. Choose your subscription plan

### **Step 2: Setup Your Property**
1. Add room inventory (room types, quantities, amenities)
2. Configure pricing and rate plans
3. Set up housekeeping schedules
4. Configure payment methods

### **Step 3: Connect Your Channels**
1. Connect OTA channels (Booking.com, Agoda, etc.)
2. Integrate payment gateways
3. Setup WhatsApp Business Account
4. Configure email notifications

### **Step 4: Train Your Team**
1. Create staff accounts with role-based permissions
2. Access training materials and video tutorials
3. Schedule onboarding sessions
4. Get support from our dedicated team

### **Step 5: Go Live**
1. Configure your online booking engine
2. Publish your hotel profile
3. Start accepting reservations
4. Monitor performance through dashboards
